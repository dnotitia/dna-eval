# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Scope: (optional) where the decision applies, if not the whole project

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraint, the evidence. Facts only.

## Decision

What we do, stated as a rule a reader can apply ("X goes in Y", "always Z").

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what to watch.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we rejected, and why, in one or two lines.
